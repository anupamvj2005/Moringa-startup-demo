
# 🌿 Moringa Kitchen
### Everyday Nutrition Made Simple

Moringa Kitchen is a food-focused startup project that aims to make moringa powder a convenient part of everyday Indian cooking. The platform showcases individual moringa products, combo packs, and recipe guides accessible through QR codes printed on product packaging.

## 📌 Project Overview

Many people find plain moringa powder difficult to incorporate into their daily meals because of its strong taste and limited recipe guidance.

Moringa Kitchen addresses this challenge by presenting moringa-based cooking products with familiar Indian flavours, easy-to-follow recipes, and a user-friendly online shopping experience.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Promote convenient use of moringa in everyday cooking.
- Showcase individual products and combo packs.
- Provide QR-code access to cooking recipes.
- Create a simple and responsive product website.
- Build customer trust through transparent product information.
- Explore opportunities for value-added agricultural products.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🛍️ Product Catalogue
Browse individual moringa products, including pure moringa powder and proposed cooking blends.

### 🎁 Combo Packs
Explore curated product combinations designed for customers who want to try multiple products.

Designed to provide a convenient browsing experience across mobile phones, tablets, and desktops.

## 💡 What Makes It Different?

- **Cooking-focused approach:** Positions moringa as an everyday food ingredient rather than only a supplement.

- **QR-enabled recipes:** Connects physical product packaging with digital cooking instructions.
- **Convenient product combinations:** Offers both individual products and combo packs.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. Customers visit the Moringa Kitchen website.
2. They browse individual products or combo packs.
3. They select a product and proceed through the available ordering process.
4. The delivered product includes a QR code.
5. Customers scan the code using a smartphone.
6. The QR code opens the relevant recipe page.
7. Customers follow the cooking instructions and explore other recipes.

## 📱 QR Code Implementation

Each product can have a unique QR code linking to a permanent recipe URL.

Example:

```text
https://yourdomain.com/recipes/moringa-dal
https://yourdomain.com/recipes/moringa-roti
https://yourdomain.com/recipes/moringa-chutney
```

Ensure that these pages exist and are publicly accessible before printing the QR codes on product packaging.
