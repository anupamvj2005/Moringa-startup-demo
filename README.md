
# 🌿 Moringa Kitchen
### Everyday Nutrition Made Simple

Moringa Kitchen is a food-focused website concept that makes moringa powder convenient to use in everyday Indian cooking. It showcases individual products, combo packs, and recipes accessible through QR codes printed on product packaging.

## 📌 Project Overview

Plain moringa powder can be difficult to incorporate into everyday meals due to its strong taste and limited recipe guidance.

Moringa Kitchen aims to address this challenge by offering cooking-friendly moringa products, familiar Indian flavours, easy-to-follow recipes, and a user-friendly shopping experience.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Make moringa easier to incorporate into everyday cooking.
- Showcase individual products and combo packs.
- Provide QR-code access to cooking recipes.
- Create a responsive and user-friendly website.
- Promote value-added moringa food products.

Browse individual moringa products, including pure moringa powder and cooking blends.

### 📱 Responsive Design
Browse the website on mobile phones, tablets, and desktop computers.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. Customers visit the website.
2. They browse individual products or combo packs.
3. They select products and place an order through the available ordering system.
4. The product package includes a QR code.
5. Customers scan the QR code using a smartphone.
6. The QR code opens the relevant recipe page.
7. Customers follow the cooking instructions and explore more recipes.

## 📱 QR Code Implementation

Each product can have a unique QR code linking to a recipe page.

Example recipe URLs:

- `/recipes/moringa-dal`
- `/recipes/moringa-roti`
- `/recipes/moringa-chutney`

Ensure the recipe pages exist and are publicly accessible before printing the QR codes on product packaging.
